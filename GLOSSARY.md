# Glossary

**Listener**: the person using MyGo Music, signed in to their NetEase account through this plugin.

**Source**: what this plugin is to the app: one provider of songs, albums, artists, playlists and an account.

**Track**: one song NetEase can play. An episode of a podcast is not a Track.

**Quality Level**: how good the audio of a Track is, from lowest to highest: Standard (标准), Higher (较高), Exhigh (极高), Lossless (无损), Hi-Res. The Listener picks Standard, High or Lossless for the whole app, and can ask for Hi-Res in place of Lossless. They get the best level at or below the one asked for that their account allows.

**Trial Clip**: the 30-second excerpt NetEase serves in place of a VIP-only Track to an account that can't play it. It is never played. The Track counts as Unavailable.

**Unavailable**: a Track the Listener can't play in full: VIP-only without VIP, region-locked, or delisted.

**Region Lock**: NetEase refusing a Track to a Listener outside mainland China. A region-locked Track is Unavailable unless the Listener sets a Mainland IP.

**Mainland IP**: a mainland Chinese address the Listener can give, so NetEase treats their requests as coming from inside China.

**Session**: the Listener's signed-in state with NetEase, started by scanning a QR code. When it expires, the Listener signs in again.

**Charts**: NetEase's ranked song lists (飙升, 新歌, 热歌, …). Signed-out Listeners see them on Home.

**Daily Recommendations**: the songs NetEase picks for the Listener each day. They lead Home.

**Liked Songs**: the Listener's 「我喜欢的音乐」 playlist, listed among their playlists. NetEase keeps it itself, so it can't be renamed or deleted.
